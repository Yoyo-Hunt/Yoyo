Point at the drones’ bolts to block them. Every deflected bolt flies back and takes out a drone. Chain deflects for a bigger combo.

Blade

Move your mouse or finger to aim. Or use A / D or the left and right arrows. Press Space to pause. The blade turns at a limited speed, so aim early.
